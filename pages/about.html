About Rosairo Gems & Lapidary

A Legacy of Sri Lankan Gemstones Since 1994

Established in 1994 in Ratnapura, Sri Lanka, the heart of the country's renowned gemstone industry, Rosairo Gems & Lapidary has been dedicated to the sourcing, cutting, polishing, and export of fine natural gemstones for customers around the world.

With decades of experience in the Sri Lankan gem trade, we combine traditional knowledge with skilled lapidary craftsmanship to deliver gemstones that meet the needs of collectors, jewellery designers, manufacturers, and gem enthusiasts worldwide.

Our Gemstone Collection

Natural gemstones selected and prepared with care.

We offer a wide selection of natural sapphires and other precious and semi-precious gemstones in a variety of sizes, colours, shapes, and qualities.

  • Natural Unheated Sapphires
  • Natural Heated Sapphires
  • Precious Gemstones
  • Semi-Precious Gemstones
  • Specially Calibrated Stones
  • Diamond-Cut Gemstones
  • A wide range of sizes and shapes to suit different jewellery requirements

Whether you are looking for a single exceptional gemstone or calibrated stones for jewellery production, our collection is available to customers worldwide.
